## How a session works

1. You pick a folder matching recent study (or `mixed` for integration).
2. Tutor gives **one problem** without naming the pattern.
3. You drive clarifying questions and approach.
4. Tutor scores /35 and gives senior-level feedback.
5. Log results in [`progress/progress.md`](../progress/progress.md).
